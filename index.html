Frequently Asked Questions about The Agentic Group

What does The Agentic Group do?

The Agentic Group is a UK AI consultancy based in West Wales that designs, deploys and manages bespoke AI systems for businesses. Their services include AI voice agents (Agentic Voice), AI chat agents (Agentic Chat), automated social media marketing (AgenticReach), AI system design, process automation, business assessments and workforce training.

What is AgenticReach?

AgenticReach is an AI-powered social media marketing automation product by The Agentic Group. It learns your brand voice, colours and messaging, then automatically schedules and publishes content across LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook and other platforms, saving businesses 8-12 hours of marketing time every week.

Does The Agentic Group offer Welsh language AI?

Yes. The Agentic Group is currently trialling a Welsh language AI voice agent specifically designed for businesses that serve Welsh-speaking communities in Wales. Contact them on 07554 430 732 for more information.

How much does an AI system from The Agentic Group cost?

The Agentic Group offers a free Business Assessment and Discovery Call to understand your needs before quoting. Their products start from £25/property/month for StayGentic. Contact them directly for a bespoke quote on 07554 430 732 or via theagenticgroup.co.uk.
